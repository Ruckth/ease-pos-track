# Ticket Tracking

This context describes how customers and staff track a reported issue from submission through resolution.

## Language

**Ticket**:
A customer-reported issue that staff tracks through a fixed workflow.
_Avoid_: Feedback, report, card

**New**:
A submitted Ticket that staff has not reviewed yet.

**Acknowledged**:
A Ticket that staff has reviewed and accepted, but has not started working on.
_Avoid_: Accepted, triaged

**In Progress**:
A Ticket that staff is actively investigating or fixing.
_Avoid_: Doing, working

**Waiting for Customer**:
A Ticket that cannot progress until the customer provides information or takes an action.
_Avoid_: Waiting, pending

**Resolved**:
A Ticket whose reported issue has been addressed; this is the terminal workflow state.
_Avoid_: Done, complete, closed
