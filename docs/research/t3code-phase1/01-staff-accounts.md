## What a staff member does

The operator provisions one Owner against an independently verified Google subject. The Owner invites a colleague by email, selects their role and explains that staff can see all Projects in this first release. The colleague opens the invitation, signs in with Google, confirms the invited mailbox and sees the team workspace. Existing invitations can be revoked or reissued. On a shared device, signing out clears protected subscriptions, cached Ticket data and the active account label. Cover invitation expiry, wrong Google account, switching accounts and provider outages in both English and Thai.

## One core, two account types

Mount one v2 core with Google, username and password components. Use one React ConvexAuthProvider and an app users table. Map a verified auth user ID uniquely to either a staffId or customerId. Google registration creates a pending identity with zero workspace access. Username signup creates a customer through the Project enrollment flow. Keep the types exclusive in this release. Staff can use a separate customer account to test the customer experience. Neither an email match nor a browser-supplied role can join the two records. Each protected handler calls getAuthUserId for the live session and checks the sign-in admission row, current generation, active principal and current role. Refresh and first use cannot create admission. Record stable domain IDs for ownership, and separate non-secret credential references for audit. The v2 JWT subject identifies the session. [Core API](https://docs.convex.dev/convex-auth-api/server/) · [Google integration](https://docs.convex.dev/auth/convex-auth/methods/oauth).

## Bootstrap, invitations and recovery

Bootstrap uses an internal, audited one-use transaction restricted to the configured deployment and exact Google subject. Require zero active Owners, bind the authenticated pending identity and consume the grant atomically. The team should enroll a second Owner with independent recovery before general rollout. Concurrent demotion or deactivation must preserve at least one active Owner. A sole-Owner lockout goes through the named deployment operator and out-of-band identity verification. Record the replacement binding, revoke old access and preserve staffId.

Use the Google component's validated profile.id and emailVerified fields at enrollment. Require an exact invited address and a fresh, expiring code to that mailbox bound to the pending user and invitation. Deliver staff invitation proof through the identity flow with its own sender configuration. Use the separate Resend identity sender before invitations. Use a random, hashed invitation secret and redeem only after sign-in and confirmation. Atomically reject expired, revoked, reused or role-edited invitations. Recheck the binding on retries. Returning staff resolve by their bound identity. Sign-in recovery uses Google account recovery. Moving a staff binding to a different Google account requires independent Owner/operator verification, revocation and an audit record. [Google verification](https://developers.google.com/identity/gsi/web/guides/verify-google-id-token).

## Permissions and deactivation

Owner administers the team, Projects, membership, routing policies and Operations repair. Staff work Tickets and manage their own approved connections and notification preferences. Area membership determines assignment eligibility. The proposed global staff visibility assumes a single internal team. Adopt global staff visibility for this single-team pilot. Require Project-level staff authorization on lists, direct reads, queries, uploads, counters and writes before onboarding staff who need isolation.

On disable, atomically deny the principal, advance the credential generation, invalidate sponsored automation and revoke access to prior sessions and keys. Enabling again requires fresh authentication under a new generation. Old keys stay revoked. At dispatch, compare the sponsor and generation on outstanding work. Retain the durable staff identity for audit. Sign-out revokes the current session. A confirmed account replacement or security incident revokes all sessions and keys. Capture a local test where an already-open subscription loses access and a previously issued token fails the next mutation.
